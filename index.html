Art reference

ARMORY is a 1v1 fighting game where every fighter is a living weapon. The look, every fighter, every animation and every special.

PLAY IT AT ARMORYGAME.COM
CH. 1

The look

A hand-inked manga fighting game: fun, loud and readable on a phone screen. Big moments turn into manga panels.

Brush ink

Black outlines with varying weight, thicker on the outside and thinner inside.

Flat colour

Flat cel fills with one hard shadow. No soft gradients or glows.

The weapon IS them

They never hold a weapon. The weapon part is about 40% of their height, with the face on it.

Reads tiny

Chunky silhouettes so you can tell who's who at phone size.

Screentone for FX

Screentone, speed lines and splatter go in effects and panels, not on the characters.

Real objects

The weapon should work like the real thing (a musket has a hammer, a trigger and a barrel in the right places).

Main colours

Ink Paper Steel Gold Razor red Bastion plum Forge orange
